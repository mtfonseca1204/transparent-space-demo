Principles

Paper, not screen.

Four principles guide every decision — from the mark on the cover to the smallest tooltip in product. When in doubt, fall back to these.

P·01

Measure twice.

The product measures things. So does the brand. Every spacing value, every stroke weight, every type size sits on the same numerical grid. Nothing is eyeballed.

P·02

Ink on paper.

Warm carbon (#0A0906) on warm paper (#F2F1EC). Or its inverse. No blue, no gradient, no drop shadow. The ramp between is for hierarchy, not decoration.

P·03

Mono speaks.

JetBrains Mono carries the brand voice — labels, numbers, captions, the wordmark itself. Inter handles long-form reading. Switch with intent.

P·04

Hard edges.

2px corner radius maximum. Hairlines are 1px. Strokes are 1.5px. Registration brackets reappear everywhere as a quiet motif — the system stamping itself.

The mark is the manifesto

Three squares, hair-thin outlines, perfectly stacked. You can see through them — that's the entire pitch. Hold the mark to that standard and the rest of the system follows.

Logo · construction

Three squares.

The mark is built parametrically: three identical squares, offset along the diagonal, drawn as outlines only. The geometry never changes — only the size.

Everything on 4.

Logo · misuse

Don't.

The mark holds up because the geometry never moves. Six things that break it.

DO · master form

Outline at 1.5px on ink. The reference.

DON'T · fill in

No filled ramp. Outline only.

DON'T · rotate

The diagonal is the diagonal. Don't tilt it.

DON'T · recolor

Ink or paper. Never brand colors.

DON'T · stretch

Squares are squares. Lock the ratio.

DON'T · shadow

No depth. The mark sits flat on the surface.

End of system · vol.1

Questions, edge cases, or a request for a new lockup? File a brand request — keep the system honest. We update the volume number, never the rules.
